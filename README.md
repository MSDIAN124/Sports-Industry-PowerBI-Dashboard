# Esports vs Field Sports Industry Analysis

A Power BI dashboard comparing revenue, audience, sponsorship, and in-game sales across the Esports and Field Sports industries.

## Problem Statement

Sports and entertainment stakeholders need to understand how Esports compares with traditional Field Sports in terms of revenue streams, audience size, and sponsorship potential.

This project explores four key questions:

1. Which industry generates more revenue?
2. Which industry has a larger audience?
3. How do sponsorship and in-game sales differ?
4. Which games/sports and regions drive the most revenue?

## Dataset

- **Source:** Synthetic Excel dataset created for portfolio purposes, representing the Esports and Field Sports industries.
- **Key fields:** Industry, Game/Sport, Year, Region, Category, Revenue, Audience, Sponsorship, and In-Game Sales.
- **Data preparation:** Standardized industry names, corrected data types, and validated measures in Power BI.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — data cleaning.
- **Microsoft Power BI** — data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard development.

## Key Insights

1. **Highest total revenue — Field Sports**
   - Revenue: **$18.5 billion**

2. **Highest revenue growth — Field Sports**
   - Growth in 2026: **5.7%**, reported as 1.6 percentage points higher than in 2025.

3. **Larger audience — Field Sports**
   - Audience: **32.6 billion**

4. **Higher sponsorship revenue — Field Sports**
   - Sponsorship amount: **$47 billion**
   - Sponsorship share: **79%**

5. **Highest in-game sales — Football, Field Sports**
   - Revenue: **$11.5 billion**

6. **Top region by revenue — Asia-Pacific**
   - Industry: **Field Sports**
   - Revenue: **$6.7 billion**

7. **Average player age**
   - Esports: **22.8**
   - Field Sports: **31.7**

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Prioritize Field Sports, particularly Football in Asia-Pacific

Field Sports leads the reported figures for total revenue ($18.5 billion), audience (32.6 billion), and sponsorship share (79%). Football accounts for $11.5 billion in in-game sales, while Asia-Pacific is the top region by revenue at $6.7 billion. The analysis recommends focusing marketing budgets, partnerships, and product development on Football-related initiatives in Asia-Pacific.

### 2. Develop targeted Esports strategies for younger audiences

Esports has a lower average player age (22.8) than Field Sports (31.7). The project recommends exploring youth-oriented content, mobile-first experiences, and emerging titles to build engagement and brand loyalty.

### 3. Build on Field Sports sponsorship while diversifying revenue streams

Given Field Sports' reported 79% share of sponsorship revenue, the project recommends strengthening sponsor relationships through multi-year deals and co-branded activations. It also suggests exploring Esports revenue streams such as in-game purchases and digital merchandise.
